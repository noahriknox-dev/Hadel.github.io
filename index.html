One puzzle a day. Same puzzle for everyone.

How to play

Four number tiles. One target. Combine tiles two at a time with +, −, ×, or ÷ until only one tile is left.

Tap a tile, tap a second tile, then pick an operator. If the last tile left matches the target, you win the day.

Everyone gets the same puzzle, once a day. Miss a day and your streak resets.
